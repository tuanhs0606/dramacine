DramaCine Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DramaCine collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our app, website, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email, password, profile details.
  • Usage data: titles you watch, search queries, watch history, device events.
  • Device data: device model, OS version, language, IP address, advertising identifiers.
  • Payment data: processed by third-party payment providers; we receive limited transaction metadata.
  • Sign-in with Google or Facebook: if you choose to sign in this way, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider. We never receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and app usage statistics collected through Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics).

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Personalize recommendations and content.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Communicate updates, offers, and support messages.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud or abuse.

3. Sharing of Information

  • Service providers acting on our behalf (hosting, analytics, payments).
  • Content partners and licensors as needed to deliver content.
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

  • We use cookies, SDKs, and similar tools to remember preferences, measure performance, and personalize content.
  • You can adjust your device or browser settings to limit these technologies.

5. Data Retention

  • We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service and meet legal obligations.
  • You may request deletion of your account at any time.
  • See our Data Deletion page for how to delete your account and data: data-deletion.html

6. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

  • The service is not directed to children under 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent.

8. International Transfers

  • Your information may be processed outside your country, subject to appropriate safeguards.

9. Security

  • We use industry-standard measures to protect personal data.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Links to third-party services are not governed by this Privacy Policy.
  • Review their privacy practices separately.

11. Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
  • Material changes will be notified through the service.
